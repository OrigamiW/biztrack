Privacy Policy

BizTrack respects your privacy. This project is a small business management tool that stores information locally in your browser.

What data we store

BizTrack may store products, orders, expenses, language preferences, and cookie consent choices using your browser's localStorage.

How we use your data

The data is used only to make the app work, such as displaying your products, orders, expenses, dashboard totals, and selected language.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored locally in your own browser. BizTrack does not send this data to a server or share it with third parties.

Cookies and consent

BizTrack does not use tracking cookies. It uses localStorage to remember your cookie consent choice and language preference.

Your control

You can clear your data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or localStorage.
